# 📊 Call Center Performance Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing customer service operations of a USA-based call center. The report provides insights into call volume, response efficiency, customer sentiment, communication channels, and geographic distribution.

---

## Dashboard Preview

![Call Center Dashboard](Assets/Dashboard.png)

---

## Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard was developed to monitor the operational performance of a call center during October. It presents key customer service metrics through interactive visualizations, enabling performance evaluation and business decision-making.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Total Call Duration** | Daily call duration in minutes to monitor operational workload. |
| **Customers by Reason** | Analysis of customer inquiries by billing, payments, and service outages. |
| **Response Time (SLA)** | Service performance categorized into Within SLA, Below SLA, and Above SLA. |
| **Responses by Channel** | Customer interactions across Call Center, Chatbot, Email, and Web. |
| **Total Calls by Day** | Weekly distribution of incoming call volume. |
| **Call Duration by Sentiment** | Total call minutes grouped by customer sentiment categories. |
| **Customer Distribution** | Geographic distribution of customers across U.S. states. |

---

## Key Insights

- Billing questions represent the highest customer inquiry category (**23K**).
- **21K** customers were served within the defined Service Level Agreement (SLA).
- Friday recorded the highest call volume (**5.6K**).
- The Call Center channel handled the largest number of customer interactions (**11K**).
- Negative sentiment generated the highest total call duration (**279K minutes**).
- Customer activity is concentrated across the eastern and central regions of the United States.

---

## Dashboard Features

- Daily call duration analysis
- SLA performance monitoring
- Customer inquiry segmentation
- Multi-channel response analysis
- Weekly call volume tracking
- Sentiment-based performance evaluation
- Geographic customer distribution map
